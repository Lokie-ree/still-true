Residential Lease Agreement

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the “Lease”) is made and entered into on the first day of the month by and between NORTHFIELD PROPERTY HOLDINGS LLC, a limited liability company (hereinafter “LANDLORD”), and the undersigned occupant or occupants (hereinafter “TENANT”), for the premises described in Section 1 below. In consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows.

1. Premises and Term

LANDLORD leases to TENANT, and TENANT leases from LANDLORD, the dwelling unit identified in the Occupancy Schedule attached hereto, together with the fixtures and appliances therein and the right to use those common areas designated for resident use. The initial term of this Lease shall be twelve (12) consecutive months commencing on the Commencement Date stated in the Occupancy Schedule. TENANT shall not occupy the premises prior to the Commencement Date without the prior written consent of LANDLORD.

Upon expiration of the initial term, this Lease shall convert to a month-to-month tenancy upon the same terms and conditions, except as to rent, unless either party has given written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 7. This Lease does not automatically renew for a further fixed term of twelve months, and no provision of this Lease shall be construed to create such a renewal.

2. Rent

TENANT shall pay to LANDLORD as rent for the premises the monthly sum stated in the Occupancy Schedule, payable in advance and without demand, setoff or deduction, on or before the first day of each calendar month at the address designated by LANDLORD in writing. Payment shall be made by electronic transfer, certified funds, or such other method as LANDLORD may from time to time designate. Cash shall not be accepted at the premises under any circumstances.

Rent not actually received by LANDLORD on or before the fifth (5th) day of the month in which it is due shall be considered delinquent, and TENANT shall pay a late charge of Two Hundred Fifty and 00/100 Dollars ($250.00) in addition to the rent then owing. The late charge is not a penalty but represents a reasonable estimate of the administrative costs incurred by LANDLORD in processing a delinquent payment. Acceptance by LANDLORD of a late payment, with or without the accompanying late charge, shall not constitute a waiver of LANDLORD’s rights as to any subsequent delinquency.

Any payment returned unpaid by TENANT’s financial institution shall be treated as though no payment had been made, and TENANT shall pay a returned-item charge of Thirty-Five and 00/100 Dollars ($35.00) in addition to any applicable late charge.

3. Security Deposit

TENANT shall deposit with LANDLORD the sum stated in the Occupancy Schedule as a security deposit, to be held as security for the faithful performance by TENANT of every covenant of this Lease. The security deposit is not rent, is not to be applied by TENANT toward the final month’s rent, and does not limit TENANT’s liability for damage or unpaid amounts exceeding the deposit.

Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the termination of this Lease and the surrender of possession of the premises by TENANT, LANDLORD shall return the security deposit to TENANT, less any amounts lawfully withheld, together with an itemized written statement describing each amount withheld and the reason for withholding it. TENANT shall furnish LANDLORD with a forwarding address in writing at or before the surrender of possession; failure to do so does not extend the period stated in this Section but may delay actual delivery.

4. Utilities

TENANT shall be responsible for arranging and paying for electricity, natural gas, telephone, internet and cable television service to the premises, and shall place such accounts in TENANT’s own name as of the Commencement Date. LANDLORD shall furnish and pay for water, sewer and refuse collection service, except where separate metering has been installed, in which case those services shall likewise be the responsibility of TENANT.

5. Access by Landlord

LANDLORD, and LANDLORD’s agents and contractors, may enter the premises to inspect the same, to make necessary or agreed repairs, decorations, alterations or improvements, to supply necessary or agreed services, or to exhibit the premises to prospective or actual purchasers, mortgagees, tenants, workers or contractors.

Except in the case of an emergency, or where TENANT has abandoned or surrendered the premises, LANDLORD shall give TENANT at least four (4) hours written notice before entering, and shall enter only at reasonable times. Notice may be delivered by hand, posted conspicuously upon the entry door of the premises, or sent to the electronic mail address TENANT has provided to LANDLORD in writing.

6. Maintenance and Repairs

LANDLORD shall maintain the structural elements of the premises, the roof, the exterior walls, the foundation, and the plumbing, heating, electrical and air-conditioning systems in good repair and in compliance with applicable housing codes, except where the need for repair arises from the act, neglect or omission of TENANT, TENANT’s household or TENANT’s guests.

TENANT shall be responsible for the cost of replacing light bulbs, furnace filters, smoke-detector batteries and refrigerator water filters; for clearing any drain or toilet stoppage occurring within the premises; for repairing damage to walls, floors, doors, windows, screens, blinds and appliances beyond ordinary wear and tear; and for any repair the need for which arises from TENANT’s misuse or negligence. TENANT shall report any condition requiring repair promptly and in writing, and shall be liable for the additional damage caused by any delay in reporting it.

7. Termination and Notice

Either party may terminate the month-to-month tenancy described in Section 1, or give notice of non-renewal of the initial term, by delivering written notice to the other party not less than ninety (90) days before the intended date of termination. Notice given by TENANT shall be effective only if it is signed by every adult TENANT who executed this Lease.

TENANT’s vacating of the premises before the end of the initial term does not terminate TENANT’s obligation for rent for the remainder of that term, subject to LANDLORD’s duty to make reasonable efforts to re-rent the premises. Upon termination for any reason, TENANT shall remove all personal property, return every key and access device issued, and leave the premises in the condition in which it was received, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

8. Use of the Premises

The premises shall be used solely as a private residence for the persons named in the Occupancy Schedule. TENANT shall not assign this Lease, sublet the premises in whole or in part, or permit occupancy by any person not named in the Occupancy Schedule for more than fourteen (14) consecutive days, without the prior written consent of LANDLORD, which consent LANDLORD may withhold in its reasonable discretion.

TENANT shall not make or permit any noise or activity that unreasonably disturbs the quiet enjoyment of other residents, shall comply with all posted community rules, and shall not keep any animal upon the premises except as expressly permitted in a separate written addendum signed by both parties.

9. Default

If TENANT fails to pay rent when due, or fails to perform any other covenant of this Lease, LANDLORD may deliver written notice specifying the default. If a monetary default is not cured within seven (7) days, or a non-monetary default within fourteen (14) days, of delivery of such notice, LANDLORD may terminate this Lease and pursue every remedy available at law or in equity, including recovery of possession and of all amounts then owing.

10. Miscellaneous

This Lease, together with the Occupancy Schedule and any written addenda signed by both parties, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes every prior negotiation, representation or agreement, whether written or oral. No modification of this Lease shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties. If any provision of this Lease is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. The failure of either party to insist upon strict performance of any provision shall not be construed as a waiver of that provision or of any other.

All notices required under this Lease shall be in writing and shall be deemed delivered upon personal delivery, upon deposit in the United States mail with postage prepaid and addressed to the party at its last known address, or upon transmission to the electronic mail address most recently provided by that party in writing.
