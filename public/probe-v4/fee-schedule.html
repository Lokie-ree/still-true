Northfield Residences — Schedule of Resident Charges

Charge Amount When it applies, and what happens if it is not paid
Monthly rent As stated in the Occupancy Schedule Due in advance on the first day of each calendar month, without demand or invoice.
Late payment charge $75.00 Assessed on any rent not received in full by the end of the fifth day of the month, and assessed once per month regardless of the number of days the payment remains outstanding.
Returned payment charge $40.00 per instrument Assessed when a payment is returned unpaid by the resident's financial institution for any reason. After two returned payments in any twelve-month period, management may require certified funds for the remainder of the term.
Security deposit One month's rent Held for the term of the tenancy. Returned, less lawful deductions and with an itemised statement, within thirty days after the resident surrenders possession and provides a forwarding address.
Pet deposit $300.00 per animal Required before any animal is brought onto the premises. Refundable to the extent no animal-related damage is found at move-out.
Monthly pet rent $35.00 per animal Charged with monthly rent for the duration of the tenancy. Not refundable and not applied against the pet deposit.
Unauthorised animal charge $250.00, plus $25.00 per day Assessed when an animal is kept on the premises without a signed animal addendum, from the date management gives written notice until the animal is removed or an addendum is executed.
Lock change or re-key $95.00 per cylinder Charged when a re-key is requested by the resident, or when management re-keys following a lost key or a change in occupancy.
Lockout service $50.00 during office hours; $85.00 outside office hours Charged when staff are called to admit a resident who is locked out. Payable at the time of service.
Replacement key or fob $25.00 per key; $75.00 per fob Charged when a key or access credential is not returned at move-out or is reported lost during the term.
Parking permit $20.00 per month per vehicle Required for any vehicle parked in a resident space. A vehicle without a current permit may be towed at the owner's expense after twenty-four hours' notice.
Utility administration fee $8.50 per month Charged for the billing and allocation of water, sewer, and trash service, in addition to the allocated cost of the utilities themselves.
Trash valet service $28.00 per month Mandatory for all occupied units and charged whether or not the service is used. Bins left in a corridor outside collection hours are subject to the corridor obstruction charge.
Corridor obstruction charge $50.00 per occurrence Assessed when personal property, including refuse containers, is left in a common corridor after written notice to remove it.
Smoking violation $500.00 per occurrence Assessed for smoking of any substance inside a unit or within twenty-five feet of a building entrance. A second occurrence is a material breach of the lease.
Early termination fee Two months' rent Payable when a resident ends the tenancy before the end of the initial term. Payment of the fee does not waive rent accrued before the surrender date.
Notice to vacate No charge Written notice of at least sixty days before the end of the term is required to end the tenancy. A resident who vacates on less notice remains liable for rent through the end of the notice period.
Month-to-month premium $200.00 per month Added to monthly rent for any period the tenancy continues after the initial term on a month-to-month basis.
Lease transfer or reassignment $400.00 Charged when management approves a transfer of the lease to another qualified resident. Approval is at management's discretion and is not guaranteed.
Unit transfer within community $350.00 Charged when a resident in good standing moves to another unit in the community before the end of the term.
Move-in administration fee $175.00 Charged once at the commencement of the tenancy. Not refundable and not applied against rent or the security deposit.
Application fee $60.00 per adult applicant Charged for credit and background screening. Not refundable regardless of the outcome of the application.
Resident-caused maintenance Cost of labour and materials, plus 15% Charged for repairs arising from misuse, neglect, or damage by a resident, an occupant, or a guest. Ordinary wear and tear is not charged to the resident.
Drain clearing $135.00 per occurrence Charged when a stoppage is caused by an item introduced into the drain by a resident, an occupant, or a guest. No charge when the cause is a system defect.
Air filter non-compliance $45.00 per occurrence Assessed at inspection when the filter supplied by management has not been installed. Residents are responsible for replacing the filter each quarter.
Smoke detector tampering $150.00 per device Assessed when a detector is found disabled or with its battery removed. Repeated occurrences are a material breach of the lease.
Common area reservation $100.00 refundable deposit Required to reserve the community room. Returned within fourteen days of the reservation if the space is left undamaged.
Storage locker $45.00 per month Charged for an assigned storage unit, subject to availability. Contents are not insured by management.
Renters insurance non-compliance $45.00 per month Charged for any month in which the resident does not maintain the liability coverage required by the lease and does not provide evidence of it on request.
Notice of entry No charge Management gives at least twenty-four hours' written notice before entering an occupied unit, except in an emergency or where the resident has requested the service.
Statement of account No charge for the first two per year; $15.00 thereafter Provided on written request within ten business days.
Payment plan administration $50.00 per plan Charged when management agrees in writing to accept rent in instalments within a month. A plan does not suspend the late payment charge for the month it covers.
Collection referral Cost of collection, including reasonable attorney fees Charged when a balance remains unpaid thirty days after written demand and the account is referred to a third party for collection.
Changes to this schedule Not applicable Management may amend this schedule on thirty days' written notice to residents. An amendment does not apply to a charge already assessed.
About this page. It is a test fixture belonging to still-true. It is published because a document that is entirely a table is the shape most likely to break a receipt: every citation is a row, so every quote has to carry enough of its row to be checkable on its own. A quote that reads only “$75.00” is true and useless, and finding that out on a real reply is what this page exists to prevent.

Northfield Residences does not exist, no resident is charged anything here, and no line in this table binds anyone. Do not rely on it or mistake any figure in it for law. The amounts were chosen to be unambiguous, not typical.
