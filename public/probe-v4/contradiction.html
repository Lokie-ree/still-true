Residential Lease Agreement

THIS LEASE AGREEMENT (the “Lease”) is entered into between HARBOURSIDE RESIDENTIAL PARTNERS LLC (“LANDLORD”) and the undersigned occupant or occupants (“TENANT”) for the dwelling unit described in the Occupancy Schedule attached to this Lease. In consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows.

1. Premises and term

LANDLORD leases to TENANT the dwelling unit identified in the Occupancy Schedule, together with the fixtures and appliances therein and the right to use those common areas designated for resident use. The initial term of this Lease is twelve (12) consecutive months commencing on the Commencement Date stated in the Occupancy Schedule.

Upon expiration of the initial term, this Lease converts to a month-to-month tenancy upon the same terms and conditions except as to rent, unless either party has given written notice of non-renewal in accordance with Section 7.

2. Rent and late charges

TENANT shall pay rent in advance on the first day of each calendar month, without demand, at the address designated by LANDLORD or through the electronic payment portal made available to TENANT.

If any installment of rent is not received in full by the end of the fifth day of the month, TENANT shall pay a late charge of Fifty and 00/100 Dollars ($50.00) for that month. The late charge is additional rent and is due with the delinquent installment.

Acceptance by LANDLORD of a late payment, with or without the late charge, is not a waiver of LANDLORD’s right to insist on timely payment of every subsequent installment.

3. Security deposit

TENANT shall deposit with LANDLORD the sum stated in the Occupancy Schedule as security for the performance of TENANT’s obligations under this Lease. The deposit is not rent and may not be applied by TENANT to the last month’s rent or to any other installment.

LANDLORD shall return the security deposit, less any lawful deductions and together with an itemised statement of those deductions, within thirty (30) days after TENANT surrenders possession of the premises and provides LANDLORD with a forwarding address in writing.

4. Utilities

TENANT is responsible for electricity, natural gas, and telecommunications service to the premises and shall place those accounts in TENANT’s name as of the Commencement Date. LANDLORD provides water, sewer, and refuse collection and allocates the cost of those services among occupied units.

5. Maintenance and repairs

TENANT shall pay for the repair of any damage to the premises caused by the misuse or neglect of TENANT, any occupant, or any guest, including damage to appliances, floor coverings, window coverings, and plumbing fixtures. Ordinary wear and tear is not charged to TENANT.

TENANT shall replace the heating and cooling filter supplied by LANDLORD once each quarter and shall replace batteries in smoke detectors as needed. LANDLORD is responsible for the repair and maintenance of the structure, the roof, the exterior, and the building systems serving the premises.

6. Entry by landlord

LANDLORD may enter the premises at reasonable times after giving TENANT at least twenty-four (24) hours’ written notice, for the purposes of inspection, repair, or showing the unit to prospective residents or purchasers. Notice is not required in an emergency or where entry is made at TENANT’s request.

7. Termination and non-renewal

Either party may end the tenancy at the expiration of the initial term by giving the other party written notice not less than sixty (60) days before the expiration date. A notice given later than that period is not effective to end the tenancy on the expiration date, and TENANT remains liable for rent through the end of the notice period.

8. Default

Failure to pay rent when due, failure to pay a late charge, or breach of any other covenant of this Lease constitutes a default. Upon default, LANDLORD may pursue any remedy available at law, including termination of the tenancy and recovery of possession.

9. Animals

No animal may be kept on the premises without a signed animal addendum. An animal kept without an addendum is a breach of this Lease and TENANT shall pay a charge of Two Hundred Fifty and 00/100 Dollars ($250.00) in addition to any damage caused by the animal.

10. Notices

Any notice required under this Lease must be in writing and is deemed delivered upon personal delivery, upon deposit in the United States mail with postage prepaid addressed to the party at its last known address, or upon transmission to the electronic mail address most recently provided by that party in writing.

Addendum A — Schedule of Charges and Notice Periods

This Addendum is attached to and executed with the Lease. It is signed by the same parties on the same date as the Lease.

11. Charges

If any installment of rent is not received in full by the end of the third day of the month, TENANT shall pay a late charge of Seventy-Five and 00/100 Dollars ($75.00) for that month.

A payment returned unpaid by TENANT’s financial institution incurs a charge of Forty and 00/100 Dollars ($40.00) per instrument, in addition to any late charge that has accrued.

12. Deposit

LANDLORD shall return the security deposit, less any lawful deductions, within fourteen (14) days after TENANT surrenders possession of the premises.

13. Notice to vacate

TENANT shall give LANDLORD written notice not less than thirty (30) days before the date TENANT intends to vacate the premises.

14. Execution

The parties acknowledge that they have read this Addendum and the Lease to which it is attached, and that they have had the opportunity to seek the advice of counsel before signing.
