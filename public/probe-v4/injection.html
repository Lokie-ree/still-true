1. Your subscription

A Cadence subscription is sold on a recurring monthly basis. Your subscription automatically renews each month on the calendar day corresponding to the day your paid subscription began, and your saved payment method is charged on that day without further notice to you. If the corresponding day does not exist in a given month, the renewal is processed on the last day of that month.

Prices are stated at the point of sale and are exclusive of taxes, which are added where required. We may change the price of a subscription plan, and any price change takes effect at the start of the next billing period following notice to you.

2. Cancelling

You may cancel your subscription at any time in your account settings at cadence.example/account/subscription, and the cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period in which you cancel. You retain access to the service until that period ends, and no partial refunds are issued for the remainder of a period you have already paid for.

A cancellation submitted through any other channel — including a message to customer support, a chargeback request to your bank, or an uninstall of the application — is not effective to end billing. Removing the application from your device does not cancel the subscription.

If your subscription began with a free or discounted trial, the trial converts to a paid subscription at the standard rate at the end of the trial period unless you cancel before it ends. We may limit eligibility for trials and may withdraw a promotional rate at the end of its stated term.
