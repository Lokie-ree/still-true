## 2. The guarantee is stronger than the last review said — and the public wording is weaker

**Correction to an earlier reading.** `excerpt` was thought to weaken the
grounding claim because the model proposes clause text. It does not.
`verify()` calls `excerpt(lines[lineNo - 1], claim.support_quote)` and `excerpt`
returns **`whole.slice(start, end)` of the document line**. The model's string is
used only to locate the cut. If it is not found in the line character for
character, it is discarded and the whole line publishes. The model's text never
reaches a reader.

So *"neither I nor the model wrote that sentence"* is defensible **about the
quote**.

**What is not defensible is the sentence currently published in two places.**
`extract.ts`'s prompt says `Never copy document text into "answer"` — so the
plain-English answer **is** the model's prose. Two claims say otherwise:

- `README.md:25` — "The model never writes the answer text."
- `src/App.tsx:337` (footer) — "the model returns a line number and never writes
  the sentence."

Read against the `answer` field, both are false. This is the class of defect this
project has retracted three times, it is on the surface a judge reads first, and
it was **on camera in beats A and D**.

**Fix in both places, same pass.** Suggested footer text:

> The plain sentence above each quote is the model's summary. The quote is not:
> the model returns a line number, and the sentence is cut out of your document
> by index on the server. A quote that is not in the document cannot be shown.

## 6. Board redesign — direction, decided

The page currently reads as a scaffold with real data in it, and the hierarchy
works against the product: **the model's summary is the bold text and the quote,
which is the entire claim, is small grey italic.**

**Concept: a legal exhibit, not a SaaS page.** Paper stock; editorial serif for
anything that came out of a document; line numbers in a real gutter down the left
edge like a pleading page. The quote becomes the largest text on the card, the
answer becomes the annotation above it, and **a refusal renders with an empty
gutter** — no line number, because there is no line. The layout states the thesis
before anyone reads a word.

**Decisions:**

- **Type:** Newsreader for document text, Public Sans for chrome, IBM Plex Mono
  for indices and stamps. Public Sans is the US Web Design System's face, which
  is quietly right for a product that quotes government model documents. Mono is
  doing a real job (line numbers, hashes, timestamps), not signalling
  "technical."
- **Colour:** warm paper, warm ink, one accent — and the accent belongs to
  absence. Answers get no colour; they earn attention through size. This inverts
  green-is-good and puts the differentiator on top visually.
- **Light only.** The current `prefers-color-scheme` variant means the shooter's
  OS theme decides what beats A and D look like. Removing it removes an
  on-camera variable.
- **One staggered card entrance.** Cards already reorder as findings resolve,
  which is why the script says do not reload on camera. A stagger turns that
  reflow into choreography.
- Header paragraph drops to one line (it currently restates the og description).
  Footer takes the §2 correction.

**Invariants that must not move:**

- `refusalLine` and `receiptKey` stay **imported** from `reply.ts`. The board is
  a second renderer; that import is what closed H5 and M7 on it.
- `questionFor` stays imported from `questions.ts`.
- Refusals stay first inside a card; refusing documents stay hoisted by CSS
  `order` derived from the data, not by a hand-picked id.
- `PublicDocument` stays `Omit<Doc<"documents">, "watchError">`.
- The "quotes and counts, does not interpret or advise, not legal advice"
  disclaimer stays.
- **No new factual claims on the page.** Every sentence added is a sentence
  `reconcile.sh` will have to keep true.

Stack is already in place: Tailwind v4 via `@tailwindcss/vite`, tokens in
`src/index.css` under `@theme`. Webfonts go in `index.html` with preconnect. The
09-02 note in `index.css` deferring the typeface choice to P3 can come out — the
interface is being designed now, so it is no longer guessing.

**Scheduling:** this rides the deploy in §3. No `PARSER_VERSION` involvement, so
the freeze is safe. It must land **before** shooting, since beats A and D put the
board on camera.
